The Inventory · الجرد · Inventaire

Summary of the inventory section of MAGIS · baseline 1.1 (final) · 24 November 2026 · nothing built yet
In one sentence. What the centre owns, where it is, in what state and what it is worth — shown as a map of the centre with photos, kept by the keeper, linked to the books (every purchase of furniture or equipment) and to the calendar (the same spaces, what is always in each room).

The rules

1
Its own door, like the calendar, in the same database as the centre.
2
One map of the centre, kept once: the calendar's areas and spaces. A tick says if a space can be booked; the store and offices are only in the inventory.
3
Every purchase on a furniture or equipment line becomes an item, or is marked "not an item".
4
Value = what it cost in USD, or an estimate. No depreciation.
5
Nothing is deleted: an item leaves with a reason (given away, sold, thrown), no money.
6
Small things only as a group ("Mugs ×40"). Things that are used up stay in the books; the stock (مخازن) comes later.

The keeper is the accountant, or a person given the task (store keeper). The Superior can change every tick.

The screens

The mapThe first page: areas, a card per space with a photo, how many items, value, last count, what needs attention. Figures: purchases to register, to repair, with staff.
A spaceThe photos of what is in it, the list, count this space, move items, the sheet for the door.
An itemPhoto, details, its whole history, and its links to the books (purchase, bill, project) and to the calendar.

What an item carries

Label (QR), name, category, space, quantity, brand, model, serial number, state (good / to repair / broken), origin, purchase and supplier, value, photos, note, history. A group (Chairs ×60) splits when part of it moves: ×30 here (HO-0102-A), ×30 there (HO-0102-B).

Each category has three ticks: serial number expected · valuable (a photo and a label are asked) · shown in the calendar (as "always in the room").

Laptops with staff

An item can be given to a staff member, chosen from the HR list (or typed). It shows who has it and since when. When a staff member leaves, the keeper is reminded of what they still have.

Links

💵 The booksPurchases become items. What a project bought: a report for the donors — the items bought on the lines funded by that project, in a period, as a PDF with photos and an Excel.
📅 The calendarThe same spaces. "Always in the room" is read from the inventory. Equipment asked by a booking: a warning if there is not enough.

Phone and paper

Phone (the full MAGIS app): count with the labels, record items, report a problem. Printed: the sheet of a space for its door, the QR labels, a list for counting.

Later

  • The stock (مخازن) for materials and for the farm.
  • All the centres on the central server, with a filter by centre.

The steps of the building

  1. One map shared with the calendar; categories with their ticks; groups that split.
  2. The new screens: map, space, item.
  3. Laptops with staff; problems reported.
  4. Printed pages and the donor report.
  5. Door, roles, guide.

Each step is tried in the side pane and kept; no installer until Fouad says "build". The 0.12 inventory screens wait meanwhile.
