# For the next build (after 0.12.0) — list made 22 November 2026

Committed on `main` after the tag `v0.12.0`. Nothing is in an installer. One new database update (63).

## My colour (every person)
- At the bottom of the menu: **🎨 My colour**. Each person chooses the colour of the menu and the buttons (eight calm ready colours, or any colour),
  and a softer page background (warm paper, soft green, soft blue, light grey, dim). It shows at once while choosing; *Cancel* puts it back.
- Kept on the person's own login (migration 63: two columns on the person), so it **follows the person on every computer**. The Superior's
  colour for the house (Admin → Appearance) stays the default for everybody who has not chosen. Another person's look is never changed.
- Not on the phone window's menu yet; the Coordinator's and the directory accounts have no personal colour (they are not people of a database).
