One week in Homs, on paper

Fourteen ordinary situations, walked through with the rules of the baseline. The names are invented (Example choir, Person A…); the kinds of situation are the ones you described. Each card says what the program does, and what to try in the clickable model (calendar-model.html). Where a situation showed a gap, it is a question at the end (R1…R8).

Before the week: setting things up

1The programme of the term is typed in

  1. In September the Coordinator adds "English course", kind Course, leader Teacher A, Classroom 3, every Monday and Wednesday 17:00–19:00, from 5 October to 16 December.
  2. The program makes the 20 dates. Our activities are put in first, so they hold their spaces before outside groups ask.
  3. Same for the club, Christian education (Saturday), the prayer group (Thursday).
Model: the coloured blocks of the week (English course, Photography club, Christian education, Prayer group). Click one: "Every Monday, Wednesday · 17:00–19:00 · 20 dates".

2A choir asks for Hall 1 every Tuesday evening

  1. Reception chooses Outside booking → organisation "Example choir" (registered once: person in charge and phone come by themselves).
  2. Hall 1, 18:00–20:00, every Tuesday until the end of January. Price: only the Director sees and sets it.
  3. The program checks each Tuesday. All free → saved, "to confirm" until the choir confirms.
Model: Tuesday 18:00 in Hall 1, "◆ Example choir". See as Reception: the price is hidden; see as Team: the person and phone are hidden.

3A weekly booking where one date is already taken

  1. Someone asks Hall 1 every Wednesday 10:00–12:00. On Wednesday 25 November our drawing workshop is there.
  2. The space is never given twice: that date cannot be saved. The program lists it and offers "Save without this date".
Model: + New → Our activity → Hall 1, a Wednesday before the 25th, 10:00–12:00, Every week → Save.
R1: save without the taken dates, or refuse the whole series?

Monday

4Reception opens the laptop at 8:30

  1. The Today strip: 1 group arrives, 1 leaves, halls in use now, bedrooms free tonight, 3 "to confirm".
  2. To prepare today: Classroom 3 before 17:00 (18 people); Bedrooms 4–6 for the scouts arriving; Bedrooms 1–3 ready again after the parish youth leave.
Model: the five tiles at the top, and the button "To prepare today".
R6: what time do groups usually arrive and leave? The list could then say "Bedrooms 4–6 before 14:00".

5The parish youth leave, the scouts arrive

  1. Reception marks the youth "left" and the scouts "arrived". The strip changes; the bedrooms of the youth go into "ready again".
  2. The bar of the scouts shows "arrived".
Model: see as Reception, click the scouts' bar (Bedrooms 4–6) → Mark arrived.

6A school phones: Hall 2 on Thursday morning, not sure yet

  1. Reception books it "to confirm" (dashed). It already holds Hall 2: nobody else can take Thursday 9–12.
  2. If the school never answers, the hall stays held.
R2: does "to confirm" hold the space? Until a date, after which the program reminds you?

Tuesday to Thursday

7The workshop coordinator wants Hall 1 on Tuesday at 19:00

  1. The choir is there 18:00–20:00. Not possible. Outside bookings already agreed are never moved.
  2. The coordinator chooses another hall or another hour.
Model: + New → Our activity → Hall 1, Tuesday 24, 19:00–21:00 → "Not possible: the space is already held".
R3: between two bookings in the same hall, do you need time to prepare (for example 30 minutes)?

8A parish rents Kitchen 1 for a lunch

  1. Outside booking, Kitchen 1, Wednesday 10:00–16:00. It appears in "To prepare today" on Wednesday.
R5: is a kitchen booked by hours like a hall, or for the whole day?

9A retreat group of 6 asks for two bedrooms, Wednesday to Saturday

  1. Bedrooms 1 and 2 are free from Wednesday night (the youth left Monday). The stay is saved; the bars run over the three nights.
  2. Bedrooms free tonight goes down by two from Wednesday.
R4: a bedroom with 3 beds and a group of 2: can another group use the third bed, or is the room taken?

10The price agreed with the choir changes

  1. The Director changes the price from 20 to 25 USD. The booking keeps a history line: old, new, who, when.
  2. Payments already received stay; the accountant sees the new suggestions in the books.
Model: see as Director, click the choir → Change the price → the history appears.

Friday to Sunday

11A private meeting on Friday afternoon

  1. The meeting room is booked with the private tick. Phones, the TV and the Team see only "Reserved".
Model: see as Team, Friday 15:00 in the Meeting room.

12An association wants Hall 2 on Sunday

  1. A gentle warning: "Sunday: usually no bookings". Reception may still save it.
Model: + New → Outside booking → Hall 1, Sunday 29, 14:00–16:00 → warning → "Save anyway".

13The Christmas festival takes the whole centre

  1. The Director books the whole centre on Saturday 12 December. It cannot be saved while anything holds a space that day.
  2. Christian education (Saturday 10–12) is ours: its date of 12 December is cancelled with the reason "Christmas festival"; the other Saturdays stay. Outside bookings that day would have to be dealt with by phone first; they are not moved by the program.
Model: Month → December: the festival on the 12th. Click Christian education on a Saturday: "Dates off: Sat 12 Dec (Christmas festival)".

14August, the centre is closed

  1. A coloured band on every day of August. A group asking for a stay gets a warning; booking stays possible.
  2. At the end of the month, the report by organisation: how many times each one booked, days and hours, which spaces, ours against outside.
Model: Month → go to August 2027: the band "Summer closing".
R7: who sees "To prepare today"? R8: the TV screen: from what hour to what hour is it on?
