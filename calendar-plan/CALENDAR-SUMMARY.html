The Calendar · التقويم · Calendrier

Summary of the calendar section of MAGIS · baseline 2.1 (final) · 24 November 2026 · nothing built yet
In one sentence. One calendar per centre, inside MAGIS, that tells everybody — from the director to the people who prepare the halls — what happens today, where, and what is free: our own activities and the bookings of others, on the laptop, on the phone and on a screen at the entrance.

The golden rules

1
One calendar for our programme and for outside bookings (halls, guest stays, kitchens).
2
A space is never given twice. If a space is already held at that time, the second booking cannot be saved. An outside booking already agreed is never moved; our activities come first because we put them in first.
3
"To confirm" already holds the space, until a date written in the booking (7 days by default); after it the program reminds you, it never frees the space by itself.
4
Looking is for everyone, changing is for a few. No approval step: whoever has the right does it.
5
Only inside the centre. Nothing on the internet. The phone keeps the view of its last connection.
6
Simple words. We "prepare" a hall or a room. Nothing is deleted: it is cancelled with a reason and kept. Every change is kept (who, when, what) and the Director sees it.

"Team" is everyone else who only looks, including leaders of activities who have no account (they may have a calendar phone). There is no accountant role: the accountant sees the payments as suggestions in the books. The Superior can change every tick.

Three ways to see it

LaptopA "Calendar" door in MAGIS. Day, week, month; search; the Today strip; book by dragging over hours or days.
Calendar phoneLinked once by a manager, with a name and a role. Opens with a short code, no sign-in. Read only. Keeps 1 month back and 6 months ahead.
TV at the entranceToday, by space and hour, 08:00–22:00. A first plain version; the real design (logo, photos, animation) later.

What the calendar holds

SpacesAreas (with a colour) → spaces: halls, classrooms, kitchens, library, outdoor… and bedrooms with their beds. Two quick views: Halls and Rooms; more categories in Settings (Playground…). One group per bedroom. A warning when more people than a space holds. A space can be closed for repairs from a date to a date.
Our programmeWorkshop, course, club, training, event, Christian education, prayer groups (an editable list). A leader, once, several days or every week; one date can be moved or cancelled. Activities outside the centre (a trip, a camp) appear without a space.
Outside bookingsHall bookings and guest stays for registered organisations (church, parish group, association, school…), with one or several persons in charge (one chosen for each booking). One or several spaces. May repeat every week.
The daysOpen 7 days. Sunday: a gentle warning for halls. Closed periods (August): a band and a warning. The whole centre can be booked for a festival.

A booking, step by step

  1. Select on the calendar: drag over the hours (day view) or the days (week, month), over one or several spaces. Or press + New.
  2. Who: our activity, or an outside booking for an organisation (its person and phone come by themselves).
  3. Where: one or several spaces. When: one day, several days, or every week on chosen days.
  4. Equipment needed from the inventory (a warning if there are not enough at that time) and services (meals, coffee break…).
  5. Price (Director or Coordinator): a "free" tick, ticked by default hides the price; untick it for an amount per person per night, for the whole booking, or each date, in USD or SYP. Reception's bookings are saved "price not set": the Director or the Coordinator decides.
  6. Status: to confirm (held until a date, 7 days by default) or confirmed; private tick if needed.
  7. The program checks: a space already held → not possible (for a weekly booking: saved without the taken dates, which are listed).

Money and paper

  • Each outside booking shows its price, the agreed total (price + services), what was received and what is still due.
  • Record a payment (Director or Coordinator) at any time, even before confirming: an advance is possible, never required. Each payment goes to the books as a suggestion, with its receipt number.
  • Cancelled after money was received: keep all, give all back, or keep a part; what is given back goes to the books as a suggestion.
  • The receipt (وصل استلام) as in 0.12: the two logos, the lines adjustable, the currency and rate, the preview before export — improved: the amount in words, a receipt for each payment (same number as in the books), places to sign, a "copy" mark, and a booking sheet in the same style.
  • Printed pages: a hall's week for its door, a short page for a day or a week, a booking's sheet for the group, an Excel list.

Every morning

The Today stripArrive today · leave today · halls in use now · rooms free tonight · to confirm. Each tile opens its list, with its buttons (mark arrived, book a free room, confirm…).
To prepare todayMade by the program, nobody types it: halls and kitchens before their first booking (people, equipment), bedrooms for the groups arriving and after the groups leaving.

Reports

As today, plus by organisation (how many bookings, days and hours, which spaces), ours against outside and the free use of our spaces. Excel export.

Display

Arabic: right to left; English or French: left to right. The week starts on Monday in every language; digits 0–9. Reminders are optional, each person switches them on (off at first).

  6. To prepare today.
  7. TV (plain).
  8. Door, roles, reports, guide.
